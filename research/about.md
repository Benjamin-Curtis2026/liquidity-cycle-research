Doug Curtis is an Associate Broker who leads The Curtis Team at Keller Williams Heritage in Kerrville, Texas. His work spans residential and commercial transactions, investment-property underwriting, and 1031 exchanges across the Texas Hill Country and San Antonio markets.

This site is independent research into how central-bank liquidity and long-term trend structure shape returns in crypto and technology-led equity markets. Each study states its question, data, and method in advance, reports results against a baseline, and lists its limitations. The site rebuilds every weekend from public data, so the figures on these pages are always current rather than a one-time snapshot.

**Data.** Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED) for balance-sheet, rate, and yield series; Yahoo Finance for adjusted daily prices; the Federal Reserve Board for FOMC dates.

**Tools.** Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib), built and published automatically with GitHub Actions and GitHub Pages. The code was developed with the help of an AI coding assistant; research questions and interpretation are the author's.
